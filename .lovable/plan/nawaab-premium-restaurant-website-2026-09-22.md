# NAWAAB premium restaurant website

## What I’ll build
- A complete responsive site with Home, About, Menu, Specialties, Gallery, Contact, and Reservation pages.
- A floating centered-logo navigation, mobile menu, footer, and working links across every page.
- A cinematic dark espresso, ivory, muted-gold, and maroon design system with editorial serif headings.
- Replaceable, clearly identified image panels throughout, keeping Biryani visually dominant.
- Working menu category scrolling, gallery filters and lightbox, review carousel, and validated contact and reservation forms.
- Direct phone, email, WhatsApp, Google Maps, menu, gallery, and reservation actions with no dead controls.
- Route-specific search and social metadata, local restaurant details, and structured restaurant data.

## Page structure
- **Home:** Biryani-led opening, editorial Biryani story, specialty mosaic, signature dishes, cinematic banner, visual food story, gallery preview, reviews, visit details, map, and reservation form.
- **About:** Short visual story, philosophy, and kitchen imagery.
- **Menu:** Biryani-first editorial menu, sticky category navigation, and scalable item lists.
- **Specialties:** Biryani-led feature story followed by Tandoor, Indian, and Indo-Chinese highlights.
- **Gallery:** 20 replaceable mixed-ratio image panels, category filters, and full-screen browsing.
- **Contact:** Restaurant details, actions, map, hours placeholder, contact form, and reservation link.
- **Reservation:** Dedicated validated table-booking form with success confirmation.

## Visual asset approach
- Use reusable premium image placeholders labelled with stable identifiers such as `hero-biryani`, `signature-biryani`, and `restaurant-interior`.
- Give each placeholder rich editorial composition, warm lighting treatment, and its intended replacement label without introducing unrelated stock imagery.

## Technical details
- TanStack Start routes with shared React/TypeScript components.
- Semantic Tailwind v4 tokens in the global design system; responsive layouts and accessible focus states.
- Lightweight CSS motion with reduced-motion support.
- Frontend-only form state and validation, ready for later backend integration.
